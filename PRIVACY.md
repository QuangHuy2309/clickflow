# ClickFlow Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 31, 2026_

ClickFlow is a browser extension that automates clicks and typing on web pages, using step plans that you create yourself.

## Data we collect

**None.** ClickFlow does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal or usage data. It contains no analytics, no tracking, and no advertising, and it makes no network requests.

## Data stored on your device

The plans you create, including their steps, selectors, data tables, and settings, are saved in your browser's local extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`). This data never leaves your device. Removing the extension deletes it, and you can also delete individual plans in the extension.

That storage is not encrypted, so please don't put passwords or other sensitive information in your plans.

## Website access

ClickFlow asks for access to a website only when you choose to record, run, or pick elements on it. While you're recording, it notes which elements you click and what you type, and saves those as steps on your device. Password and payment fields are never recorded. It reads and changes a page only to carry out the steps you start yourself, and nothing is sent anywhere. You can remove site access at any time from `chrome://extensions`.

## Limited Use

ClickFlow's use of information complies with the [Chrome Web Store User Data Policy](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/), including the Limited Use requirements.
